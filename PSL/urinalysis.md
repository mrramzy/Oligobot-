**🔬 DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL: URINALYSIS & FLUIDS (CLSI)**

**PHASE 1: SEDIMENT ANALYSIS**
- **Scan:** Scan at low power (10x) for casts and general distribution.
- **Examine:** Examine at high power (40x) for detailed identification of cells, casts, crystals, and organisms.
- **Quantify:** Report elements per low-power field (LPF) for casts or per high-power field (HPF) for cells.

**PHASE 2: CLINICAL CORRELATION & CRITICAL VALUES**
- **Critical Finding:** If Pathological Casts (e.g., RBC casts, WBC casts, broad waxy casts) are found, this is a critical result.
- **Action:** Correlate with chemistry results. Advise immediate review and suggest renal function tests (BUN/Creatinine).
- **Guideline:** Follow CLSI GP16-A3 guidelines for reporting.
