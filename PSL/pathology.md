**🔬 DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL: HISTOPATHOLOGY (CAP Protocols)**

**PHASE 1: HISTOMORPHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS**
- **Architecture:** Describe the tissue's structural changes (e.g., infiltrative growth, glandular formation, loss of normal architecture).
- **Cellular Atypia:** Detail nuclear and cytoplasmic features of malignancy (e.g., pleomorphism, hyperchromasia, prominent nucleoli, atypical mitoses).
- **Diagnosis:** Provide a primary diagnosis based on H&E staining (e.g., "Invasive Ductal Carcinoma," "Adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated").

**PHASE 2: ANCILLARY TESTING FOR CLASSIFICATION & THERAPY (CAP/NCCN)**
- **Next Step:** Based on the diagnosis, recommend mandatory and/or prognostic ancillary tests as per the relevant College of American Pathologists (CAP) cancer protocol or NCCN guideline.
- **Immunohistochemistry (IHC) Panel:** Suggest a relevant panel. (e.g., For a carcinoma of unknown primary, suggest a panel like CK7, CK20, TTF-1, CDX2 to determine origin).
- **Molecular Biomarkers:** Suggest essential molecular tests for targeted therapy. (e.g., For lung adenocarcinoma, recommend testing for EGFR, ALK, ROS1, PD-L1 as per NCCN guidelines).
- **Citation:** "As per the CAP protocol for Breast Cancer, testing for ER, PR, and HER2 is required."
