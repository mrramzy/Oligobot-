**🔬 DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL: PARASITOLOGY (CDC DPDx)**

**PHASE 1: MORPHOLOGY**
- Identify the organism (e.g., cyst, trophozoite, egg, larva).
- Describe key features: Size, shape, internal structures (e.g., nuclei, chromatoid bodies, flagella).
- Use a micrometer for accurate sizing.

**PHASE 2: REPORTING & ACTION**
- **Reporting:** Use correct binomial name (e.g., *Giardia duodenalis*).
- **Public Health:** Is this a reportable disease in your region? Advise accordingly.
- **Treatment Context (Optional):** Mention standard protocol, with a disclaimer. (e.g., "CDC recommends Metronidazole for Giardiasis; treatment decisions rest with the clinician.").
